## Methodology established

The design formalises five pillars: detection, delimitation, specificity,
qualification and continuous empirical validation. Measurements, diagnostics and
qualification are distinct; observation and segment qualification remain
independent; uncertainty remains explicit; and no composite score is adopted.

The methodology includes overlapping diagnostic episodes, cumulative-discrepancy
research, absolute and contextual diagnostics, legitimate counterexamples,
reactive VeloViewer comparison, prospective tile-hunting studies, positive
visitation audits and controlled policy replacement.

## Owner planning amendments incorporated

- G3B.3A inventories and consolidates existing measurements before proposing new
  implementation.
- Every independent diagnostic uses the same five-part evaluation framework.
- Rolling, event-driven and near-zero-reset discrepancy methods remain separate
  candidates with interactive owner review.
- Multi-scale research covers observation-count and elapsed-time windows and is
  retrospective; real-time use is deferred.
- Episode semantics include unknown boundaries, evidence interruptions and
  unresolved activity-terminal episodes.
- Independent diagnostic evaluation is separate from later independent
  qualification-policy evaluation, with partitioning required before holdout use.
- G3B.3 is divided into seven separately commissioned, accepted and owner-gated
  increments, G3B.3A through G3B.3G.

## Tensions retained for owner review

- Conservative tile evidence requires affirmative support, while lack of support
  alone must not manufacture rejection.
- Context improves interpretation, but disturbed context can create a misleading
  local baseline.
- External and prospective comparators are useful without being absolute truth.
- Parameter exploration is necessary, but parameter selection risks overfitting
  the small reviewed calibration set.
- Continuous refinement is required, while historical evidence and independent
  evaluation boundaries must remain immutable.

## Validation

The methodology maps all five pillars, cites supporting and adverse evidence,
preserves unreviewed and uncertain states, retains sample-only conservative tile
visitation, and specifies reactive, proactive and positive-evidence validation.
No implementation, threshold, schema, pipeline, Explorer or governed-label
change is included.

direction does not itself authorise G3B.3A or any later increment.
